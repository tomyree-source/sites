Guide by Concern

Stiff shoulders & neck

For neck and shoulders locked up by desk work and smartphones

In traditional East Asian medicine, a neck and shoulders stiffened by long hours in the same posture are seen as stagnation in the flow of qi and blood. We warm and release the area from the shoulder blades to the neck with oil, easing the muscles that support the weight of the head, and combine this with beauty seitai to address rounded shoulders.

Related meridians: Bladder, Gallbladder, Small Intestine

Lower back pain & heaviness

For both standing and desk-based work

The lower back is considered closely connected to the Kidneys in East Asian medicine, and heaviness tends to appear there when cold and fatigue build up. We carefully release the lower back, hips and backs of the thighs, and use seitai to balance the pelvis area.

Related meridians: Bladder, Kidney, Governing Vessel

Swelling & heavy legs

For those whose shoes feel tight by evening

Swelling from retained fluid is linked in East Asian medicine to a weakened Spleen function. Working from the calves to the thighs along the lymph and meridian pathways, we encourage drainage and leave the legs feeling lighter.

Related meridians: Spleen, Kidney, Bladder

Sensitivity to cold

Cold hands and feet, or over-cooling from air conditioning

Feeling cold is a sign of poor circulation. Using warmed oil, we slowly release the whole body, with particular attention to the abdomen, lower back and ankles.

Related meridians: Kidney, Spleen, Triple Burner

Posture & rounded back

For rounded shoulders or an arched lower back

Beauty seitai begins by checking your postural habits. Opening the chest and balancing the back and pelvis can change the impression of how you stand. We also advise on everyday body use from a personal trainer’s perspective.

Related meridians: Bladder, Governing Vessel, Conception Vessel

Light sleep & lingering fatigue

For days when rest never feels like enough

East Asian medicine views fatigue that will not lift as an imbalance between the Heart and the Kidneys. We release the whole back in a slow rhythm and add head care, helping the body rest deeply. Aroma also helps the mind switch off.

Related meridians: Heart, Kidney, Bladder

Facial puffiness & dullness

For a clearer, fresher jawline

Stagnation in the head, neck and décolleté also affects how the face looks. Carefully releasing the décolleté, neck and head encourages circulation and a fresher impression. We recommend combining this with neck and shoulder care.

Related meridians: Stomach, Gallbladder, Large Intestine

Discomfort around your period

For heaviness and irritability that follow your cycle

Changes around the menstrual cycle are linked in East Asian medicine to the Liver. We warm and release the abdomen and lower back, and work mainly on the leg meridians to restore flow. Aromas are chosen gently to suit how you feel that day. *This is not a medical treatment. If symptoms are severe, please consult a medical professional.

Related meridians: Liver, Kidney, Spleen

Whole-body fatigue & refresh

For days when you simply need a reset

When the whole body feels heavy rather than one specific area, we structure the session to lift circulation throughout. Using the full 90 minutes on the back, legs, arms and head, it is a complete reset — like taking a deep breath under a starry sky.

Related meridians: All meridians, in balance

FAQ

I heard the therapist is male. I feel a little anxious…

The therapist is male, and we state this clearly in advance. During treatment your body is carefully covered with towels and your privacy is respected at all times. This is a professional wellness salon and no sexual services are offered. Please ask us anything before booking if you have concerns.

The address is not listed. Where is the salon?

To protect our guests’ privacy, the address is not published. The salon operates in a rental salon in Ashiya, Hyogo. Once your booking is confirmed, we will send you the address and directions individually.

What is the nearest station?

The nearest stations are Hankyu Ashiyagawa Station, JR Ashiya Station and Hanshin Ashiya Station. The salon operates in a rental salon in Ashiya, Hyogo, and the detailed address and directions from the nearest station are shared only with guests whose booking is confirmed. When booking, let us know which station you will use and we will send the clearest directions.

Can I use a shower?

Shower facilities vary depending on the rental salon used. If you wish to use one, please ask when booking.

Can I receive acupuncture?

We do not perform acupuncture at this salon. The therapist holds a national acupuncture license and applies that knowledge of East Asian medicine to the oil massage and beauty seitai.

Are there any charges beyond the ¥10,000 for 90 minutes?

No. The single course is 90 minutes for ¥10,000 (tax included) with no extra charges. Consultation and essential-oil blending are all included.

What payment methods do you accept?

As the salon is opening soon, details of payment methods will be announced on this page when it opens.

Is it OK if this is my first time?

Of course. We begin with a consultation about your condition and concerns, and adjust pressure throughout. If you prefer no fragrance, treatment with unscented carrier oil only is also available.

What happens on the day of the treatment?

After you arrive, we first hold a consultation about your condition, concerns and scent preferences. You then change before the treatment, which is 90 minutes combining oil massage and beauty seitai. After the treatment, we explain your body’s condition and self-care for everyday life.

Does the treatment hurt? Can the pressure be adjusted?

Pressure is adjusted throughout the treatment, so please speak up at any time if it feels too strong or if anything concerns you. We also ask about your condition and concerns during the consultation, so you can mention anything you are worried about in advance.

What should I bring, and what should I wear?

We do not specify any particular items to bring. The flow on the day is a consultation after you arrive, then changing before the treatment. Shower facilities vary depending on the rental salon used; if you wish to use one, please ask when booking.

Can I book on the same day?

As the salon is opening soon, we are not yet accepting bookings. The start of bookings, including how same-day bookings will be handled, will be announced on this page when the salon opens.

What happens if I am late or need to cancel?

The salon is fully reservation-only, open Monday to Saturday, 17:00–22:00 (closed Sundays), with the last booking timed so treatment ends by 22:00. Specific arrangements for lateness and cancellations will be announced on this page when the salon opens. As the salon is opening soon, bookings are not yet being accepted.
