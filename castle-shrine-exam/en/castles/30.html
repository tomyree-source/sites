About Takato Castle

Renovated by Takeda Shingen as a base for controlling southern Shinano, it was where Nishina Morishige faced the Oda army in 1582. In the Edo period it became the seat of the Takato domain, and today it is famous for its Takato kohigan cherry blossoms, said to number about 1,500 trees.

What to look for

A castle ruin park famous for cherry blossoms, and the reconstructed Tonya Gate and Shintokuden
