About Nagashino Castle

A castle built by Suganuma Motonari and the stage of the Battle of Nagashino in 1575. Okudaira Nobumasa, besieged by Takeda Katsuyori's large army, held out in the castle until relief arrived from the allied Oda–Tokugawa forces. Earthen ramparts and dry moats remain, and it is a National Historic Site.

What to look for

The earthen ramparts and dry moats of the main bailey site, and the Nagashino Castle Ruins Preservation Museum
