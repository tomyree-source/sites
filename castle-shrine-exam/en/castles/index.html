The 100 Famous Castles of Japan

The 100 Famous Castles of Japan (Nihon 100 Meijo) is a list selected by the Japanese Castle Foundation, spanning Hokkaido to Okinawa — from the Ainu chashi forts of Nemuro to Shuri Castle in the Ryukyu Kingdom. This guide covers every castle on the list in English: where it is, who built it, what survives, and what to look for when you visit.

Collecting the stamp at each castle is a popular way to tour Japan. When you are ready, test what you have learned with the free Castle Exam — the app interface switches to English.
