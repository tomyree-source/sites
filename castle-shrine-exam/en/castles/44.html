Nagoya Castle

EraConstruction began in 1610
TypeFlatland castle
Main keepMain keep lost to wartime fire; the main bailey palace has been reconstructed

About Nagoya Castle

The residence of the Owari Tokugawa family, which Tokugawa Ieyasu ordered the western daimyo to build. The great keep, topped with golden shachihoko, burned down in an air raid in 1945. The reconstruction of the main bailey palace was completed in 2018, and it is open to the public together with its screen paintings.

What to look for

The reconstructed main bailey palace, and the surviving corner turrets and Omote-Ninomon gate (Important Cultural Properties)
