Inuyama Castle Passes 700,000 Annual Visitors for the First Time

This is a news article bringing you the latest developments concerning castles and shrines. Source: the Chunichi Shimbun (November 19, 2026). The annual admissions of Inuyama Castle, a National Treasure in Inuyama City, Aichi Prefecture, passed 700,000 for the first time in history on November 19. A ceremony was held before the castle keep, and Mizumi Suzuki (25), a nurse visiting from Fukuoka City who became the 700,000th visitor, was congratulated.

【From Over 650,000 at the End of October to the Milestone in Just Three Weeks】By the end of October, admissions at Inuyama Castle had already passed 650,000, the highest figure for this time of year (NHK, November 2, 2026). The increase was seen as driven by growing numbers of visitors from overseas, and it was reported that if the pace continued, annual admissions would pass 700,000 for the first time. As expected, the milestone was reached on November 19 — just about three weeks after the castle was reported to be heading for 700,000 at the end of October.

【Why Are People Flocking to Inuyama Castle Now?】Behind the growth in admissions is an increase in visitors from overseas (NHK, November 2, 2026). In the '2026 Castle Annual Trend Access Ranking' announced on November 18 by Riksta, the operator of 'Oshiro ga Iine,' Inuyama Castle ranked third in eastern Japan, up from seventh the previous year. Spreading the word on social media is also said to be a topic of conversation. In the castle town, efforts to make use of vacant houses and other buildings are also progressing, widening the flow of people who enjoy the castle and its town together.

【For Exam Study】For exam study, the key point is how to look at a surviving castle keep. The keep of Inuyama Castle is a National Treasure, and it is said to be among the oldest of the surviving keeps. It stands on a hill overlooking the Kiso River, a castle whose town is as enjoyable to walk as the castle itself. The growth in admissions can be seen as the result of the castle's own appeal combined with the walkability of the castle town and the spread of information on social media. See also the related article 'Inuyama Castle and the Kiso River Scenery: The River and Castle Town Seen from the National Treasure Keep.'

When visiting Inuyama Castle, please check the viewing information in the official guidance of Inuyama City.

A word from Tsurugi: 700,000 visitors — what an amazing number. It had only just passed 650,000 at the end of October, and now it has already broken through the milestone. Inuyama Castle's popularity looks set to keep rising.
