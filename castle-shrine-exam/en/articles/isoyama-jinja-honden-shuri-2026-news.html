Isoyama Shrine's Main Hall Gets a New Roof After 76 Years — Restored in Vermilion

This is a news article bringing you the latest developments concerning castles and shrines. Source: the Shimotsuke Shimbun (November 10, 2026). The main hall of Isoyama Shrine in Kanuma City, Tochigi Prefecture, known as a famous spot for hydrangeas, has had its repair work completed, and the hall was shown to local residents at the annual festival.

【Built in 1662 — A Main Hall Designated a Prefectural Cultural Property】The main hall of Isoyama Shrine is said to have been built in 1662 (Kanbun 2), and it was designated a Tochigi Prefectural Tangible Cultural Property in 1987. In this repair, the copper roof was re-laid for the first time in 76 years, and the exterior was also restored to a dazzling vermilion appearance. A main hall with more than 360 years of history has been cared for again by the hands of the local people.

【'A Spiritual Home for Local Residents' — For the Years Ahead】Nobuhiko Kaneko (79), the shrine's chief priest, said, 'I hope it will continue to exist as a spiritual home for local residents.' The repaired main hall was shown to local residents at the annual festival, and it greeted its new appearance watched over by the people of the community.

【For Exam Study】For exam study, the key point is how to look at a shrine's main hall and its preservation and repair. The main hall is the most important building of a shrine, enshrining the deity, and it is passed down over long years through preservation repairs such as re-laying the roof and repainting the exterior. Isoyama Shrine is also known as a famous spot for hydrangeas, a shrine where visitors can enjoy the seasonal flowers together with the shrine buildings. Now that the repair of the hall has just finished, it is a good time to see the main hall shining in vermilion.

When visiting Isoyama Shrine, please check the visiting information in the shrine's official guidance.
