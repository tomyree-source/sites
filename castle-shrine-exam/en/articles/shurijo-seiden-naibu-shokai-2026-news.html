Shurijo Castle's Seiden Interior Revealed: A Yellow Plaque and 33 Dragons

This is a news article bringing you the latest developments concerning castles and shrines. Source: FNN Prime Online (November 16, 2026) and Okinawa Television (November 16, 2026). Ahead of its public opening on November 23, the interior of the restored Seiden (main hall) of Shurijo Castle has been shown to the public. It was opened to the press on November 6 and 8, and to 129 residents of the local Shuri district on November 15.

【The 'Chuzan Seido' Plaque — Its Base Turns from Vermilion to Yellow】The plaque 'Chuzan Seido' hung above the throne has changed markedly in this restoration. Drawing on new findings from old documents, its base has changed from vermilion to yellow (ochre), and the characters are applied using the 'ukibori' technique, in which carved-out letters are pasted on, giving them a more three-dimensional feel. 'Chuzan Seido' means 'May the age ruled by Chuzan continue forever,' and the inscription is said to copy calligraphy presented by the Emperor of China. In the Heisei-era restoration, the base was painted vermilion, but later records revealed its original color, and it has now been changed to this yellow.

【The Usasuka on the First and Second Floors — An Interior Adorned with 33 Dragons】The Seiden is a three-story wooden building with a total floor area of about 1,200 square meters. On the first floor the king conducted politics and ceremonies; the second floor was a space for the royal family and court ladies, and a place to receive envoys from China. Inside, visitors can now see the 'Kaen Hoju' (flaming sacred jewel) at the back of the first-floor Usasuka (the king's seat), the plaque on the second-floor Usasuka, and the vivid 'Kinryu Goshiki no Kumo' (golden dragons amid five-colored clouds) painted on the pillars. According to Akiko Higa, a curator of the Okinawa Churashima Foundation, which manages Shurijo Castle, the dragons painted on the great dragon pillars at the front of the Seiden and on the interior pillars number 33 in total across the hall.

【Seven Years On — The Completion Ceremony on November 22, Public Opening on the 23rd】Construction of the Seiden began in November 2022, and the main building work was completed at the end of September 2026. It is a restoration achieved about seven years after the fire of October 31, 2019, which burned down seven buildings including the Seiden. A completion ceremony hosted by the national government will be held on November 22, and the Seiden will open to the public from noon on November 23. Advance reservations are required to view the interior of the Seiden.

For exam study, the key point is the interior of the Seiden and the character of the 'Reiwa Restoration.' This restoration follows the basic design of the 'Heisei Restoration,' completed in 1992, while reflecting newly discovered historical sources and research findings — the change in the plaque's color, the ukibori technique, and the deep red of the Kushijima-kiri bengara pigment are examples.
