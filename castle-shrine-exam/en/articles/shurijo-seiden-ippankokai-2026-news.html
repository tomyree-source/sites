Shurijo Castle's Seiden Main Hall Opens to the Public at Noon on November 23: Adult Admission Rises to 1,000 Yen and Timed Reservations Apply Inside the Hall

This is a news article bringing you the latest developments concerning castles and shrines. Sources: Nihon Jiji Shimbun (November 1, 2026) and announcements by Shurijo Castle Park. Restoration of the Seiden main hall of Shurijo Castle, destroyed by fire in 2019, is complete, and it will open to the general public from noon on Monday, November 23, 2026 (a national holiday). The interior of the Seiden will be by advance reservation with a designated date and time in 30-minute slots, and from the same day, admission to the paid area will be revised from 400 yen to 1,000 yen for adults. On the previous day, November 22, a ceremony marking the completion of the restoration will be held, and Shurijo Castle Park will be closed for the whole day.

According to press materials issued on October 22 by the Okinawa General Bureau of the Cabinet Office, the restoration completion ceremony will be held on November 22 from 1:00 p.m. for about one hour at a special venue in the Shurijo district. On that day, all facilities within Shurijo Castle Park, including the paid area, will be closed for the entire day. On the following day, the 23rd, Okinawa Prefecture will hold an opening ceremony from 9:00 a.m. to 9:45 a.m. at Una, the courtyard in front of the Seiden. Because of this ceremony, the free area inside the castle walls will be closed until 11:15 a.m., and the paid area until noon. The general opening of the Seiden begins at that noon.

To view the interior of the Seiden, you need to reserve an “Admission Ticket (with Seiden interior viewing reservation)” that includes a designated date and time. There is no additional charge for the time designation. Individual reservations are accepted online, and up to nine tickets can be purchased in a single payment. Tickets for entry from November 23 to December 31 have already been released in two batches, on September 1 and October 1. The free areas, such as Shureimon Gate, and the paid areas other than the Seiden interior can still be entered without a reservation, as before.

From entries on November 23, admission to the paid area will be revised as follows (according to Shurijo Castle Park): individual adults, 400 yen → 1,000 yen; high school students, 760 yen; elementary and junior high school students, 370 yen; children under 6, free. The Hokuden (North Hall), Nanden (South Hall), Una courtyard and other areas are scheduled to be restored in stages from here on.

Shurijo Castle was the center of politics, diplomacy and culture in the Ryukyu Kingdom, and restoration of the Seiden began construction in November 2022. The fire of October 31, 2019, in which seven buildings including the Seiden burned down completely, and the “visible reconstruction” approach — carrying out the work while showing the restoration process — are also topics that connect to the exams. See also the related articles “What Is Different from the Previous Restoration? The 'Reiwa Restoration' of Shurijo Castle's Seiden, Just Before Its November 23 Public Opening” and “Shurijo Castle's Seiden to Open to the Public from November 23: Restoration from the 2019 Fire Enters Its Final Stage.”

A word from Tsurugi: The restoration that took seven years finally opens with the noon bell. You need a reservation to go inside the Seiden, so if you're going, decide your date and apply early.
