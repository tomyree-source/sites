The Gods Gather in Izumo: Izumo Taisha's Kamiarisai, November 18–25, 2026

This is a news article bringing you the latest developments concerning castles and shrines. Sources: the official Izumo Taisha website (“Kamiarisai 2026”) and the Izumo Tourist Association's guide to the Month of the Gods. At Izumo Taisha in Izumo City, Shimane Prefecture, the Kamiarisai festival of 2026 (Reiwa 8) will be held from the evening of Wednesday, November 18 to the evening of Wednesday, November 25. These eight days are the heart of “Kamiarizuki,” the Month of the Gods, when the gods of all Japan are said to gather in Izumo in the tenth month of the old lunar calendar.

On the evening of Wednesday, November 18, the welcoming rite (Kami-mukae Shinji) is held at Inasa Beach (the tourist association's guide lists 7:00 p.m.). Led by the serpent deity Ryūja-jin, the gods proceed to Izumo Taisha, where the welcoming festival is held at the Haiden hall. On Thursday, November 19, the Kamiarisai rite is held at 9:00 a.m. at the Honden, followed at 11:00 a.m. by the Ryūja-jin-kō Taisai. From November 19 to 24, the evening Kagura prayers (Yo-Kagura Kitō) are held at 5:00 p.m. at the Kaguraden hall. On Monday, November 23, the Niiname-sai harvest festival, the Kamiarisai, and the Grand Enmusubi Festival are held at 10:00 a.m., and on Wednesday, November 25, the Kamiarisai and Grand Enmusubi Festival are held at 10:00 a.m., with the farewell rite (Karasade-sai) at 4:00 p.m. at the Haiden.

The Grand Enmusubi Festival is a rite that members of the public who have applied may attend, and applications for both November 23 and 25 have already reached capacity, according to the official Izumo Taisha website. The Kamiarisai rites themselves are open only to those invited by the shrine, but visitors may still worship in the precincts. The Izumo Tourist Association warns of very heavy crowds on Thursday, November 19 and from Saturday, November 21 to Monday, November 23. Traffic restrictions and free shuttle buses are also planned, so please check the latest official information before you go.

For the shrine exams, this is the scope of Chapter 5 of the textbook. The basics are three points: “Izumo Taisha enshrines Ōkuninushi-no-Ōkami,” “the tenth month of the old calendar is called Kamiarizuki in Izumo,” and “the Honden is built in the Taisha-zukuri style.” Remember, too, that the dates of the Kamiarisai change every year because they follow the old lunar calendar. In 2026, Kamiarizuki runs from Monday, November 9 to Tuesday, December 8. See also the related article “Izumo Taisha and Kamiarizuki: The Rites of the Month When the Gods of All Japan Gather, and the Shrine of Matchmaking.”

The Kamiarisai runs from the evening of Wednesday, November 18 to the evening of Wednesday, November 25. Applications for the Grand Enmusubi Festival are already full. Before you go, please check the latest information on the official Izumo Taisha website. This site's events calendar also lists the Kamiarisai dates.

A word from Suzu: Eight days when the gods of all Japan gather in Izumo — the most special time of the year. From the welcoming rite on the beach to the farewell on the last day, Izumo in autumn is full of the gods' footsteps.
