Dazaifu Tenmangu Guide: Sugawara no Michizane, the God of Learning, in Fukuoka

Dazaifu Tenmangu in Dazaifu City, Fukuoka Prefecture, is a shrine enshrining Sugawara no Michizane. Regarded as one of the head shrines of Tenmangu and Tenjin shrines nationwide, it draws students and exam candidates all year round who come to pray to the god of learning.

Sugawara no Michizane and his exile to Dazaifu — Sugawara no Michizane was a scholar and statesman of the Heian period who rose as high as Minister of the Right. But he lost a political struggle, was exiled to Dazaifu in Engi 1 (901), and is said to have died there two years later. A series of calamities in the capital after his death is told to have led to rites to pacify his spirit, and eventually to the faith that enshrined him as 'Tenjin.'

The Tenjin faith and the god of learning — Because Michizane was the foremost scholar of his age, the Tenmangu faith spread as devotion to a 'god of learning.' Dazaifu Tenmangu and Kyoto's Kitano Tenmangu are generally named as the central shrines of Tenmangu across Japan. Dazaifu's main hall, rebuilt in the Azuchi-Momoyama period, is designated an Important Cultural Property.

The Flying Plum and a shrine of plum blossoms — The 'Flying Plum' (tobiume), known from the poem Michizane composed when leaving the capital, is a legendary plum tree that flew to Dazaifu overnight out of longing for him. The plum of that legend still stands before the main hall, and the grounds are planted with many plums, beloved as a plum-blossom spot in early spring. The approach's famous sweet, umegae mochi, is also a confection told of in tales connected with Michizane.

Visiting in exam season — Centered on January, students hoping to pass their exams visit with their families in a steady stream. Ema votive plaques lined with the words of candidates have become a winter scene of Dazaifu. Learning is not achieved in a single night — Michizane's way of life is also received as encouragement to keep on learning.

Exam study points — Within the scope of Chapter 5 of the Shrine Exam textbook. Confirm the basics: 'the deity of Dazaifu Tenmangu is Sugawara no Michizane' and 'the Tenjin faith spread as devotion to a god of learning.' Persons enshrined as deities can be usefully organized alongside Ieyasu at Toshogu.

Reaching Dazaifu, the walk along the approach from Nishitetsu's Dazaifu Station is part of the pleasure. Why not pay a visit to the god of learning, combined with a report on your everyday studies?
