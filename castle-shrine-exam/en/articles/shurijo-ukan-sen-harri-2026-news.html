A Chinese Song Returns After 190 Years — Shuri Castle Celebrates Its Restored Main Hall with the 'Ukan-sen Hari' Boat Race

This is a news article bringing you the latest developments concerning castles and shrines. Source: Bijutsuten Navi (November 8, 2026). Seven years after the fire of October 2019, and ahead of the general opening of the restored main hall of Shuri Castle (from noon on November 23), an 'Ukan-sen Hari and Light-up Lighting Ceremony' was held on the evening of November 7 at Ryutan Pond in Naha, an artificial pond overlooking Shuri Castle.

In the Ryukyu Kingdom era, the 'Ukan-sen Hari' was held at Ryutan Pond to entertain the investiture envoys (sappushi) sent from China. The local NPO Shuri Machizukuri Kenkyukai recreated the lyrics as faithfully as possible based on past records and research. The basis was the record of the 'Choyo no En' banquet held in 1838. The Chinese hari song in the diary had unknown melody and pronunciation, but part of it was found to match a hari song still sung in Kume, Naha, so this time it was sung in a pronunciation close to modern standard Chinese.

At the light-up, Ryukyuan classical music was performed, and the audience cheered at the fantastic scenery and elegant singing. The Ryutan Pond light-up runs until February 27, 2027, lit from 6 p.m. to 10 p.m. Ryutan Pond, a historic stage, is a spot to revisit now that Shuri Castle's main hall has been restored.

For exam study, the key point is the relationship between the Ryukyu Kingdom and the Chinese investiture envoys. Shuri Castle was the castle of the Ryukyu Kingdom from the 15th to the 19th centuries; its main hall was designated a National Treasure in 1925, destroyed in the Battle of Okinawa in 1945, restored in 1992, and registered as a World Heritage Site in 2000. Ryutan Pond is an artificial pond beside Shuri Castle that served as the stage for entertaining investiture envoys. See also the related article 'Shuri Castle Main Hall Finally Opens to the Public at Noon on November 23 — Admission 1,000 Yen for Adults, Timed Reservations Inside the Main Hall.'

Shuri Castle's main hall opens to the public from November 23. Along with your visit, the Ryutan Pond light-up comes recommended. Before you go, please check the latest information in Shuri Castle Park's official guidance.

A word from Tsurugi: A song heard again after 190 years, echoing across the waters of Ryutan Pond. Shuri Castle's restoration is bringing back not just a building, but the memory of the kingdom itself.
