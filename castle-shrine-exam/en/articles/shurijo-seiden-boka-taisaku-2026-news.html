Seven Years After the Fire, New Protection Too — Shuri Castle's Seiden Hall Reopens with 300 Sprinklers and Surveillance Cameras

This is a news article bringing you the latest developments concerning castles and shrines. Source: QAB Ryukyu Asahi Broadcasting (November 16, 2026) and NHK (November 17, 2026). Shuri Castle's Seiden main hall, destroyed by fire in 2019 and restored, will open to the public on November 23. Before the opening, its interior was shown to the press on November 6 and 8, and to 129 local residents of Shuri on November 15. Alongside the dazzling decorations, attention also fell on the new measures to protect the hall from fire.

【300 Sprinklers — Something the Heisei-Era Hall Did Not Have】Learning from the 2019 fire, sprinklers have been newly installed — something the Heisei-era hall did not have. Inside the hall, 300 sprinkler outlets have been fitted. In addition, surveillance cameras that can record even in dark places, and equipment that detects flames, smoke, and heat, have been introduced. While recreating the historic space, the hall is now better equipped to detect a fire quickly and keep damage to a minimum if one ever occurs.

【The Cause of the Fire Is Still Unknown — Why Did It Spread?】According to NHK (November 17, 2026), the cause of the 2019 fire has still not been identified. The questions remain: why did the fire spread, and how have its lessons been applied? For the restoration, research into old documents was used, and the walls and pillars were painted with natural pigments thought to have been used in the Ryukyu Kingdom era. The wooden three-story hall opens its first-floor Shachagui and second-floor Ufugui to the public, and the plaque 'Chuzan Seido' has changed its base color from vermilion to yellow.

【For Exam Study】For exam study, the key point is fire prevention as a way of 'protecting' cultural properties. In the fire of October 31, 2019, nine facilities including the Seiden main hall of Shuri Castle were destroyed. Work on the restored hall began in November 2022, and the main construction was completed at the end of September 2026. A national completion ceremony will be held on November 22, and public opening begins at noon on November 23. Viewing the interior of the hall requires an advance reservation for a designated date and time. See also the related article 'A Yellow Plaque and 33 Dragons — Inside Shuri Castle's Seiden Hall, Revealed for the First Time.'

When visiting Shuri Castle, please check the reservation method and opening information on the official Shurijo Castle Park website. This site's event calendar also lists the 'Shuri Castle Seiden Main Hall — Public Opening Begins' on November 23.
