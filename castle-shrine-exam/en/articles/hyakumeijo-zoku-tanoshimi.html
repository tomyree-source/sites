How to Enjoy the 100 Famous Castles and the Next 100: Planning a Journey to All 200 Castles

Another article introduced how to start the Japan 100 Famous Castles stamp rally. Here we take up what lies beyond: including the Next 100 Famous Castles, 'how to enjoy 200 castles.'

【The 100 and the Next 100】The 100 Famous Castles of Japan were selected by the Japan Castle Foundation in 2006, and the Next 100 Famous Castles were additionally selected in 2017 — 200 castles in all. Their feature is that they include not only famous castles with keeps standing, but many mountain-castle sites and fortified residences that tell the history of their regions. With the combined official guidebook, you can manage all 200 castles in a single volume.

【Build Your Trips Region by Region】The realistic way to conquer 200 castles is to divide them by region. Some regions, like Shikoku with its four castles, concentrate surviving keeps; others are a run of mountain castles. When a single trip combines not only castles but the local castle town, regional food and hot springs, the journey itself becomes richer. A plan without strain is the trick to lasting long.

【How to Enjoy Castles without Keeps】Most of the 200 castles are sites where no keep remains. The core of walking them is reading the layout map and tracing traces of moats, earthen ramparts and stone walls. Books explaining how to walk earthen castles, and basic knowledge of layouts, become your strength just as they are. Visit the information boards and local museums together, and your understanding deepens at a stroke.

【Exam Study Points】'The 100 Famous Castles of Japan were selected by the Japan Castle Foundation' is knowledge that also appears on the Castle Certification exam. A stamp-collecting journey and exam study help each other. A castle you have seen on the ground should come back vividly in the exam questions.

The 200 castles are a large goal with no need to hurry. Start from a nearby region and make your travel plans. Certification knowledge will raise the resolution of your journeys.
