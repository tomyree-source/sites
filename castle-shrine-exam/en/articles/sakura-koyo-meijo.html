Castles with Cherry Blossoms and Autumn Colors: Enjoying How the Keep Changes with the Seasons

No account of how to enjoy castle touring can leave out the seasons. The same castle shows an entirely different face in the cherry-blossom season and in the autumn-colors season.

【Cherry Blossoms and Castles】Many castles across Japan are famed for cherry blossoms. Hirosaki Castle is known for the combination of blossoms with keep and moats, and its 'hanaikada' — petals floating on the moat like a raft — is a specialty. Himeji Castle, Matsumoto Castle and Kumamoto Castle, too, are known as castles many people visit in blossom season. Enjoy each contrast: a white keep against pale blossoms, a black keep against blossoms.

【Autumn Colors and Castles】Autumn castles are rich in highlights, too. Places such as Genkyuen Garden at Hikone Castle, the gardens of Nijo Castle and the moat banks of Matsumoto Castle let you enjoy the combination of autumn leaves and architecture. At mountain-castle sites, you can also savor the contrast of colored leaves with stone walls and earthen ramparts. Books that tour castles from a weather forecaster's viewpoint also tell how season and weather change a castle's expression.

【The Pleasure of Choosing a Season】If you want to avoid the crowds, aim for just before or after the blossom peak, or for a weekday morning. There are also castles known for their winter appearance, such as Matsumoto Castle or Aizuwakamatsu Castle (Tsuruga Castle) in the snow season. Making plans to visit castles season by season is itself one of the joys of castle touring. Check the latest local blossom and autumn-color information in official announcements before you set out.

【Exam Study Points】Seasonal knowledge itself is not at the center of the exam range, but basic information about famous castles — 'Hirosaki Castle = one of the twelve surviving keeps,' 'Tsuruga Castle is not a surviving keep' — is connected with Chapter 4 of the textbook. Organize it in a way that serves both trip planning and review.

A castle is not a place you finish with a single visit. Revisit in a different season and find a new face of your favorite castle.
