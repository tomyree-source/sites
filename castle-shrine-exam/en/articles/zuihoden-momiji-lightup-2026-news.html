Zuihoden's Momiji Light-Up: Date Masamune's Mausoleum Glows with Autumn Leaves until November 30

This is a news article bringing you the latest developments concerning castles and shrines. Source: The Kahoku Shimpo (November 25, 2026). A maple light-up, the 'Zuihoden Momiji Meguri Light-Up,' is being held at Zuihoden (Sendai City, Miyagi Prefecture), the mausoleum of Date Masamune, founder of the Sendai domain. The gorgeous Momoyama-style architecture and the autumn leaves can be enjoyed together. The event runs until November 30.

【Momoyama-Style Buildings Floating in Orange and White Light】 At Zuihoden, the Nehanmon Gate, the main hall and the Kansenden Hall are lit with orange and white lights, and andon lanterns line the approach and the stone steps. This year's autumn leaves are just at their best, and visitors were heard cheering at the fantastical scene of red and yellow Japanese and palmate maples woven together with the golden-glowing buildings. The light-up runs from 3:30 p.m. until closing time.

【Open until 7:00 p.m. on Weekends and Holidays, with a Samurai Hospitality Troupe】 On Saturdays, Sundays and holidays, opening hours are extended until 7:00 p.m., with installations of lanterns bearing the family crest and Japanese umbrellas. The Date Busho-tai samurai hospitality troupe also appears on set days during the period. The special night bus, the 'Momiji Light-Up-go,' also runs on November 21, 22, 23, 28 and 29.

【For Exam Study】 For exam study, the key point is that Zuihoden is the mausoleum (otamaya) of Date Masamune. Zuihoden was built in 1637 and designated a National Treasure in 1931, but was destroyed in the war. The present buildings were rebuilt in 1979. The combination of the Momoyama-style buildings, lavishly decorated in red, green and yellow against black lacquer, with the autumn leaves that reach their best from mid-November is what makes this place special. According to Sendai City, the lantern and umbrella installations and the Date Busho-tai appearances also take place during the period. The Zuihoden Momiji Meguri Light-Up runs until November 30. Our events calendar also lists it.
