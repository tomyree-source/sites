Kodaiji Temple Rebuilds Its Reception Hall — Burned Down in 1789, Reopening to the Public on November 14

This is a news article bringing you the latest developments concerning castles and shrines. Source: Jomo Shimbun (November 10, 2026). Kodaiji Temple in Higashiyama Ward, Kyoto — founded by Nene, the wife of Toyotomi Hideyoshi — announced on November 10 that it has completed the reconstruction of its reception hall (kyakuden, or small hojo), which was moved from Fushimi Castle, where Hideyoshi spent his later years, and burned down in 1789. The hall was shown to the press, and general admission begins on November 14. Kodaiji is formally a Buddhist temple (Rinzai Zen, Kenninji school).

In its Fushimi Castle days, the reception hall was where Hideyoshi held banquets with feudal lords, and its sliding-door paintings by Kano-school artists are said to have filled the rooms. With no surviving drawings, the hall was recreated as an 'imaginative restoration' based on old pictorial maps and excavation findings, reproducing the lavish appearance of the time with abundant gold-leaf wall paintings. The completed hall has six rooms, and the fusuma partitions are vividly painted with flowers, plants, and pines by the Japanese-style painter Tadashi Shimura, based on historical research.

Since 1988, the temple has been carrying out a maintenance plan to restore the temple grounds lost to fire to their original appearance, and construction on this project began in 2022. The head priest, Norinaga Goto, was deeply moved, saying the result is something he can face Hideyoshi and Nene with pride, according to the article.

Kodaiji was founded in 1606, and many of its buildings were moved from Fushimi Castle. The Kaisando Hall and the Kangetsudai moon-viewing pavilion are designated Important Cultural Properties of Japan. For exam study, the key points are the relocated buildings from Fushimi Castle, the idea of 'imaginative restoration' — reviving a building with no surviving drawings from old pictorial maps and excavation findings — and the role that Important Cultural Property designation plays in preserving buildings. As a temple connected with Hideyoshi and Nene, it is also a place that conveys the splendor of Momoyama culture.

General admission to the rebuilt reception hall begins on November 14. Before visiting, please check the latest information in Kodaiji Temple's official guidance.
