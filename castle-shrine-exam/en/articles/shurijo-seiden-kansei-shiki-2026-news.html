Shuri Castle's Main Hall Marks Its Completion Ceremony Today, Seven Years After the Fire

This is a news article bringing you the latest developments concerning castles and shrines. Sources: RBC Ryukyu Broadcasting (November 22, 2026) and Kyodo News (November 21, 2026). The main hall of Shuri Castle in Naha City, destroyed by fire on October 31, 2019, has been restored, and the national 'Shuri Castle Main Hall Restoration Completion Ceremony' is being held today, November 22, from 1:00 p.m. at Shuri Castle Park. The ceremony is attended by national and prefectural officials and is closed to the general public. Tomorrow, November 23, after an opening ceremony hosted by Okinawa Prefecture, the main hall opens to the public at noon.

【Seven Years of Restoration and the 'Visible Reconstruction'】Restoration of the main hall began in November 2022. Under the banner of 'visible reconstruction,' the process was opened to the public, with a viewing area set up at the construction site, so that visitors would keep coming. Drawing on research findings since the 1992 'Heisei restoration,' the exterior was finished in a deep red using the natural pigment 'Kushi-magiri bengara.' According to prefectural and municipal statistics, Shuri Castle Park welcomed 1.67 million visitors in fiscal 2024, about 60 percent of the pre-fire peak (Kyodo News). During this time, about 6.1 billion yen in donations was sent to Okinawa Prefecture, with support coming from across Japan and overseas (FNN Prime Online, November 22, 2026).

【Public Opening from Noon Tomorrow — Reservations and Admission】Public opening begins at noon on Monday, November 23 (a national holiday). Viewing the main hall interior requires a timed reservation in 30-minute slots; if slots remain on the day, tickets can be bought online up to 15 minutes before the entry time, or at the Kofukumon ticket office (Impress Watch). From November 23, admission to the paid area changes to 1,000 yen for adults, 760 yen for high school students, 370 yen for elementary and junior high school students, and free for children under six. Note that Shuri Castle Park is closed all day today, November 22, for the ceremony, and also closed on the morning of November 23.

【For Exam Study】For exam study, the key point is the story of the 'Reiwa restoration' of Shuri Castle's main hall. Shuri Castle was the political, diplomatic and cultural center of the Ryukyu Kingdom from the 15th to the 19th centuries, and its main hall was among the nine buildings destroyed by the fire of October 31, 2019. Restoration began in November 2022, and the main construction work was completed at the end of September 2026. The completion ceremony on November 22 followed by the public opening at noon on November 23 is a milestone of the reconstruction worth remembering. See also the related article 'Shurijo Castle's Seiden Main Hall Opens to the Public at Noon on November 23: Adult Admission Rises to 1,000 Yen and Timed Reservations Apply Inside the Hall.'

When visiting Shuri Castle, please check the reservation method and opening information in the official guidance of Shuri Castle Park. Our events calendar also lists the 'Shuri Castle Main Hall Public Opening' on November 23.

A word from Tsurugi: Seven years to reach this completion ceremony. For everyone who has watched over the castle since the day of the fire, today must be a very special day. From tomorrow's public opening, many more people will meet the red of the main hall again.
