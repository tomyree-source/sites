Hojo Ujimasa

北条氏政 (ほうじょう うじまさ)

Years1538–1590
HouseHojo clan
RegionKanto
EraAzuchi-Momoyama period

Overview

The fourth head of the Later Hojo clan. While repeating alliances and confrontations with the Uesugi, the Takeda, and the Tokugawa, he maintained his territory, but he was defeated in Toyotomi Hideyoshi's Odawara Campaign and committed ritual suicide (seppuku).
