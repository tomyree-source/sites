Ukita Hideie

宇喜多秀家 (うきた ひでいえ)

Years1572–1655
HouseUkita clan
RegionChugoku
EraAzuchi-Momoyama period

Overview

A daimyo of Bizen and one of the Five Elders (Go-tairo) of the Toyotomi government. At the Battle of Sekigahara he fought hard as a mainstay of the Western Army, and after the defeat he was exiled to Hachijo Island, where he lived to a great age.
