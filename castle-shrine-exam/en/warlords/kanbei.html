Kuroda Kanbei

黒田官兵衛 (くろだ かんべえ)

Years1546–1604
HouseKuroda clan
RegionKinki (Kansai)
EraAzuchi-Momoyama period

Overview

A military strategist who rose from a retainer of the Kodera house of Harima to serve as Toyotomi Hideyoshi's chief of staff. His real name was Yoshitaka; later he took the tonsure and the name Josui. He went to persuade Araki Murashige and was imprisoned in Arioka Castle, and he bore responsibility for the strategy of the Chugoku campaign and the conquest of Kyushu.

Life

He was born the son of Kuroda Mototaka, a retainer of Kodera Masamoto, lord of Gochaku Castle in Harima (there are various theories about his origins). Perceiving Oda Nobunaga's rise, he led his lord's house over to the Oda side and joined Hashiba Hideyoshi's Chugoku expeditionary force. In 1578 he went to Arioka Castle to persuade the rebel Araki Murashige, was captured, and was imprisoned for about a year. The trouble in his leg from this period is handed down as part of his appearance in later years. After his rescue he supported Hideyoshi in the Chugoku campaign, at the Battle of Bitchu Takamatsu Castle, and with advice such as the 'Great Return from Chugoku' after the Honno-ji Incident, and also bore responsibility for the strategy of the conquest of Kyushu. Later he was enfeoffed in Buzen and made Nakatsu his seat. At the time of Sekigahara he showed independent movement in Kyushu, and died in 1604.

In battle

Kanbei's generalship was characterized by shaping the form of a battle through diplomacy and information. Combining the opening of castles through negotiation, the securing of supply lines, and the dividing of the enemy, he aimed at a way of fighting that kept his own side's losses down. After his experience of imprisonment in Arioka Castle, he is spoken of as the man who advised hastening the pace of Hideyoshi's bid for the country. As for his raising of troops in Kyushu at the time of Sekigahara, there is a theory that sees in it ambition for the realm, and a theory that sees the preservation of the Kuroda house.

Arms and equipment

Kanbei is known as a man of intellect, and anecdotes of martial prowess do not stand out. Tales remain of the figure of him dragging the leg he injured in his imprisonment, and of his frugal way of living. As a Christian daimyo he held the baptismal name Don Simeon, and is said later to have abandoned the faith. His letters to his retainers reveal careful consideration for the handling of people.

Character and records

Although the appellation 'military strategist' (gunshi) became established largely under the influence of later novels and storytelling, his achievements as Hideyoshi's chief of staff can be confirmed in the sources as well. Anecdotes to the effect that Hideyoshi was wary of Kanbei are also handed down, and they speak to the evaluation of his intellect by those around him. He also handed over the headship of the house to his son Nagamasa early, showing the swiftness of his judgment in protecting the house.

In literature

harimanada, taikoki, kunidori

Harima-nada Monogatari is an epic novel with Kanbei as its protagonist. The protagonist of Shinsho Taikoki is Hideyoshi, but Kanbei also appears as an important figure, Hideyoshi's military strategist. Kunidori Monogatari depicts the opening of the age of Nobunaga and Hideyoshi, whom Kanbei served.
