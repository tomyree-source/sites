Satake Yoshishige

佐竹義重 (さたけ よししげ)

Years1547–1612
HouseSatake clan
RegionKanto
EraSengoku period

Overview

Head of the Satake clan of Hitachi. A fierce commander nicknamed 'Oni Satake' (the Demon Satake), he held onto his power in Hitachi while contending with the Hojo, and handed the headship of the clan to his son Yoshinobu.
