Tokugawa Hidetada

徳川秀忠 (とくがわ ひでただ)

Years1579–1632
HouseTokugawa clan
RegionKanto
EraEarly Edo period

Overview

The third son of Tokugawa Ieyasu and the second shogun of the Edo shogunate. His late arrival at Sekigahara is famous, but in the Siege of Osaka he served as commander-in-chief, destroyed the Toyotomi house, and solidified the foundations of the shogunate.
