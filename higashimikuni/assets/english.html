A quiet, private setting, so you can truly relax

With one therapist and one guest at a time, you will never run into other guests. Stop by on your way home from work and rest your body in a quiet private room — we aim to be that kind of regular, familiar place.

A seitai perspective, not just rubbing

We do more than loosen the tired spots: we check your posture, pelvic tilt, and how your muscles work together. By addressing why an area becomes stiff in the first place, we support a body that does not slip back so easily.

Eastern medicine, applied to everyday condition

Drawing on a licensed acupuncturist's knowledge, we suggest acupoint approaches matched to the season and your constitution, plus self-care you can do at home — stretching, bathing, and dietary tips.

Before Your Visit

What to bring & wear

  • Nothing in particular. We provide a change of clothes for your treatment.
  • Seitai can be received fully clothed. Comfortable, easy-to-move-in clothing helps things go smoothly.
  • Please remove jewelry and watches before your session.
  • We recommend hydrating before and after (water is provided).
  • Please arrive about 5 minutes before your appointment (arriving much earlier may find us still preparing).

Please let us know in advance

  • If you have a fever or feel unwell, don't push yourself — contact us to reschedule.
  • We cannot treat guests immediately after drinking alcohol.
  • Please tell us in advance about fractures, dislocations, acute inflammation, or recent surgery.
  • Areas with inflamed or broken skin may be avoided during treatment.
  • If you are pregnant, or are under medical care or medication (e.g., for high blood pressure), please mention it when booking.
  • If you have an implanted device such as a pacemaker, we will adjust the seitai treatment accordingly.

Cancellations & changes

As we accept only a few guests a day, please message us on LINE as soon as you know you need to change or cancel. Repeated same-day cancellations may mean we cannot accept future bookings. If we ever need to ask for a change, we will offer alternative times with priority.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the exact address not published?

For privacy, the exact address is not published on this page. Once your booking is confirmed, we send the exact address and photos of landmarks via LINE. Your therapist handles everything from greeting you at the entrance to the treatment itself, and you will never meet other guests.

Why is it men only?

Hokushindo is men-only, as it is run by a single male staff member. We do not accept female guests.

Can I book for the same day?

Yes, if there is availability. With only one therapist, slots that are already booked cannot be offered. Please check availability on LINE.

Does it hurt? Can the pressure be adjusted?

Our seitai is not the cracking kind of adjustment; it centers on pressure and stretching, worked in slowly. Pressure can be adjusted at any time — lighter, regular, stronger — so please speak up freely.

Can I get acupuncture or moxibustion?

Not at Hokushindo. We offer relaxation and seitai bodywork only. For symptoms that seem to need acupuncture, we may suggest visiting a medical facility or an acupuncture clinic.

How can I pay?

Payment is cash only, on the day. Credit cards, e-money, and QR payments are not accepted, so please bring cash. Receipts are available.

Is there a shower or parking?

There is no shower. After oil treatments, we wipe the oil off with hot towels. There is no dedicated parking, so drivers please use a nearby coin parking lot. For bicycles, please ask when booking.

Are there multi-session passes or regular plans?

We suggest a pace based on how your body changes. Prices are 60 min: ¥2,000 first visit / ¥6,000 from the second visit; 90 min: ¥3,000 first visit / ¥9,000 from the second visit. We never pressure you into anything.
