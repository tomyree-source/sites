Kyoto — Kawaramachi / Gion | Women-Only Private Salon

A Women-Only Salon in Kyoto — Oil Massage & Beauty Seitai, 90 Minutes

Stella Altair is a women-only private massage salon in central Kyoto, within easy reach of the Kawaramachi and Gion districts. Treatments combine aromatherapy oil massage with Japanese bodywork (seitai), provided by a male therapist with 30 years of experience and a national acupuncture license (acupuncture itself is not performed at this salon).

  • Course: 90 minutes — ¥10,000 (tax included, no extra charges)
  • Open Monday to Saturday, 17:00–22:00 (closed Sundays)
  • Reservation only. The address is shared after your booking is confirmed
  • Shower facilities vary depending on the rental salon used; if you wish to use one, please ask when booking.
  • This is a professional wellness salon. No sexual services are offered

Guide by Concern

Stiff Shoulders & Neck

For necks and shoulders locked by desk work and phones

In East Asian medicine, a neck and shoulders stiffened by long hours in one posture are seen as stagnation in the flow of qi and blood. In treatment, warmed oil eases the area from around the shoulder blades to the neck, loosening the muscles that carry the weight of the head. Beauty seitai adjustment for rounded shoulders is combined as well.

Related meridians: Bladder, Gallbladder, Small Intestine

Back Pain & Heaviness

For standing work and sitting work alike

The lower back is, in East Asian medicine, deeply connected with the Kidney. It is an area prone to heaviness when chill and fatigue accumulate. We ease carefully from the lower back through the hips and the backs of the thighs, and balance the pelvis area with seitai.

Related meridians: Bladder, Kidney, Governing Vessel

Swelling & Heavy Legs

For those whose shoes feel tight by evening

Swelling, where excess fluid lingers, is related in East Asian medicine to a weakening of the Spleen’s function. From the calves to the thighs, we treat along the lymphatic and meridian flow as if pressing the fluid onward, toward lighter legs. Also recommended for legs tired from walking Kyoto’s streets.

Related meridians: Spleen, Kidney, Bladder

Feeling Cold

For cold hands and feet, and air-conditioning chill

Feeling cold is a sign of slowed circulation. With warmed oil we ease the whole body slowly, with extra care for the abdomen, lower back, and ankles.

Related meridians: Kidney, Spleen, Triple Burner

Posture & Rounded Shoulders

For rounded shoulders and an arched lower back

Beauty seitai begins by checking your postural habits. Opening the chest and balancing the back and pelvis can change the impression of how you stand. From a personal trainer’s perspective, we also advise on how you use your body day to day.

Related meridians: Bladder, Governing Vessel, Conception Vessel

Shallow Sleep & Lingering Fatigue

For days when rest never quite restores you

In East Asian medicine, fatigue that will not lift is seen as an imbalance of the Heart and Kidney. The whole back is eased in a slow rhythm, with care for the head as well, toward a body that can rest deeply. Scent helps the mind switch over, too.

Related meridians: Heart, Kidney, Bladder

Facial Puffiness & Dullness

For a clearer facial line

Stagnation in the head, neck, and décolleté affects the impression of the face. Careful work from the décolleté through the neck and around the head encourages flow, toward a clearer look. Combined care for stiff neck and shoulders is recommended.

Related meridians: Stomach, Gallbladder, Large Intestine

Discomfort Around Periods

For cycle-related heaviness and irritability

Changes that come with the cycle are related in East Asian medicine to the function of the Liver. We ease the abdomen and lower back as if warming them, and balance flow mainly along the leg meridians. Scents are chosen gently to suit how you feel. *This is not medical care. If symptoms are strong, please consult a medical institution.

Related meridians: Liver, Kidney, Spleen

Whole-Body Fatigue & Refresh

For days when you simply want to reset

When the whole body feels heavy, wherever it started, we build the session to lift circulation overall. Back, legs, arms, and head — a full 90 minutes of comprehensive care, like a deep breath under a starry sky.

Related meridians: All meridians, in balance

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions we are often asked before a visit. The salon is currently preparing to open, and bookings are not yet being accepted; the start of bookings will be announced on this page.

I heard the therapist is male. I feel a little nervous…

The therapist is male, and we state this openly in advance. During treatment your body is carefully covered with towels and your privacy is given the utmost respect. This is a professional relaxation salon and no sexual services are offered. Please ask anything at all before booking if you feel unsure.

The address is not listed. Where is the salon?

To protect our guests’ privacy, the address is not published. The salon operates in rental salons in Kyoto City; once your booking is confirmed, we send you the address and directions individually. It is easy to reach from the Kawaramachi and Gion areas.

Can I use a shower?

Shower facilities vary depending on the rental salon used. If you wish to use one, please ask when booking.

Can I have acupuncture?

No acupuncture treatment is performed at this salon. Your therapist holds a national acupuncture license and applies that knowledge of East Asian medicine to oil massage and beauty seitai.

Is there any charge beyond ¥10,000 for 90 minutes?

No. The basic course is 90 minutes for ¥10,000 (tax included) with no extra charges. Counselling and essential-oil blending are all included.

Is it all right if it is my first time?

Of course. We begin with counselling about your body and concerns, and pressure is adjusted as we go. If you dislike aroma scents, treatment with unscented carrier oil alone is also possible.

Can I visit during sightseeing?

Yes. We open from 17:00, which suits a visit after sightseeing or shopping. If you have large luggage, please let us know when booking.

Can I book for the same day?

After opening, same-day bookings may be possible when there is availability, but as the salon is reservation-only we recommend booking by the day before. Hours are Monday to Saturday, 17:00–22:00 (closed Sundays). Please note the salon is currently preparing to open and bookings are not yet being accepted.

It is my first time, and I am nervous because I do not know how the visit goes

After you arrive, the visit runs in this order: (1) counselling, where we ask about your body, your concerns, and your scent preferences; (2) time to change; (3) the 90-minute treatment; (4) aftercare, where we explain the state of your body and daily self-care. Guests whose booking is confirmed receive the salon address and directions individually. The “Your Visit, Step by Step” section on this page also walks through the order.

Will the treatment hurt? Can the pressure be adjusted?

Pressure is adjusted moment by moment. If it feels too strong or too weak, please say so at any time, even during the treatment. We begin with counselling about your body and proceed from there, so first-time guests can relax.

What should I bring, and what should I wear?

There are no particular items you need to bring. On the day, you change before the treatment, and during treatment your body is covered with towels, with your privacy respected. If you have large luggage, please let us know when booking.

How can I pay?

The fee is the basic course only: 90 minutes for ¥10,000, tax included, with no extra charges. Details of payment methods will be announced on this page together with the start of bookings. Please note the salon is currently preparing to open and bookings are not yet being accepted.

What happens if I am running late, or need to change or cancel a booking?

The salon is reservation-only. Hours are Monday to Saturday, 17:00–22:00, and the last booking is timed so that treatment ends by 22:00. How late arrivals, changes, and cancellations are handled will be announced on this page together with the start of bookings. Bookings are not yet being accepted, as the salon is currently preparing to open.

Which stations are nearest?

The nearest stations are Hankyu Kyoto-kawaramachi, Keihan Gion-Shijo, and Hankyu Karasuma. The salon operates in rental salons in Kyoto City, and the exact address is shared only with guests whose booking is confirmed, together with directions. When booking, tell us which station you will use and we will send the clearest directions.
